Before you buy

Filo PLA+ questions, answered.

Will it work with my printer?

Yes if it takes 1.75 mm filament. Filo One has a ready-made Filo PLA+ profile, and most Bambu Lab, Creality, Prusa, Elegoo and Anycubic printers work too. Our compatibility table lists popular models and recommended settings.

What's the difference between PLA and PLA+?

PLA+ has additives that make it less brittle and slightly more heat-tolerant. It prints almost the same way, so you don't need to learn anything new.

How should I store it in Singapore's humidity?

Keep opened rolls in a sealed box or bag with the desiccant pack provided. If prints start stringing, dry the roll at 45–50°C for 4–6 hours.
