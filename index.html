Questions before you buy

Buying a Filo One: your questions.

Should I buy the printer on its own or a kit?

Filo One on its own is S$349. Kits cost S$379 to S$429 and add filament and project files, which works out cheaper than buying them separately. Not sure which kit? The 2-question Kit Finder will pick one for you.

What comes in a Filo One kit?

Every kit has the Filo One printer, Filo PLA+ filament with tuned settings and a pack of tested project files. The Starter and Maker Kits also include a Refila Bag for your scraps. Compare the kits.

Do I need to join Filo Club?

Not to buy a printer. Filo Club is free and gets you the Weekly Challenge files, a stamp card, a S$3 Shopee voucher and the optional Refill Plan. Join in a minute.

What is Refila?

Our take-back partner. Fill a Refila Bag with PLA scraps and empty spools, send it back, and earn Filo Points: about S$1.25 back per kg of scraps. See how Refila works.

Why do you sell on Shopee?

Shopee gives you a familiar checkout, SPayLater, buyer protection and Shopee vouchers that stack with our own codes. If you see a similar self-levelling printer cheaper in a Singapore Shopee store, message us before buying and we’ll match the printer price.
