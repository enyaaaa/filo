The hero product

Filo PLA+ 1.75 mm

A tougher, less brittle PLA that prints at everyday temperatures. It's forgiving enough for a first print and consistent enough for the hundredth.

  • 8 colours, from Desert Sand and Oat Milk to a Silk Gold finish for gifts and décor
  • Fits most FDM printers that take 1.75 mm filament
  • Vacuum-sealed with desiccant, tangle-free winding
  • Reusable spool: return it empty for Filo Points
