Before you buy

Common questions

Do I have to use Filo filament?
No. Filo One takes any 1.75 mm filament. Filo PLA+ simply comes with tested settings for this printer, and Refill Plan members get a voucher on every reorder.
Is it suitable for kids?
Yes, with an adult nearby. The nozzle and bed get hot, so keep little hands away while it prints. The Family Kit includes a printable safety checklist for children.
How loud is it in an HDB flat?
About 48 dB in quiet mode, similar to a quiet fridge. Most people run it in the living room or a study.
What if something breaks?
Every Filo One has a 12-month warranty. Message our Shopee store and we'll arrange a repair or replacement in Singapore.
Why do I check out on Shopee?
So you get the vouchers, instalments, delivery tracking and Shopee Guarantee you already use. This site helps you choose and keeps you printing; Shopee handles the payment.
