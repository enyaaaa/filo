From box to first print

Set up in 15 minutes.

Four steps, the same for every Filo printer.

  1. 0–3 min

    Unbox

    Lift Filo One out, remove the orange transport clips and attach the top frame with the four screws provided.

  2. 3–6 min

    Plug in and pair

    Scan the QR code on the quick-start card. The Filo app connects the printer to your Wi-Fi.

  3. 6–10 min

    Load filament

    Hang the spool, feed the end into the extruder and pick “Filo PLA+” in the app. Settings load automatically.

  4. 10–15 min

    Level and print

    Tap Level. The printer checks its bed in about five minutes, then starts the dino keychain. It’s done 20 minutes later.
