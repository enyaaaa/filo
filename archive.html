What each feature does for you

Specs in plain English.

Automatic bed levelling

No paper under the nozzle.

Filo One probes its own bed and sets the nozzle height, the step that makes most beginners give up. Tap Level in the app and walk away.

Direct-drive extruder, up to 300°C

It prints more than PLA.

The motor sits right above the nozzle, so it can push flexible TPU for phone cases and grips, and handle PETG for tougher parts.

Textured PEI plate, magnetic

Prints pop off when it cools.

Lift the plate off, flex it and the print releases. No scraping, no glue stick, and the texture hides the bottom layer.

Power-loss resume + runout sensor

A tripped breaker won’t ruin a 4-hour print.

If power cuts out or the spool runs dry, Filo One pauses and saves its place. Fix it, tap Resume and it carries on.

About 48 dB in quiet mode

Quiet enough for an HDB living room.

Roughly a quiet fridge. Leave it printing in the study while you’re on a call or asleep.

300 mm/s peak, 150 mm/s typical

Most prints finish while you’re still excited.

We quote both numbers because peak speed is only part of the story. At everyday settings a keychain still takes about 20 minutes.

Before you buy

Filo One questions, answered.

Do I have to use Filo filament?

No. Filo One takes any 1.75 mm PLA, PLA+, PETG or TPU. Filo PLA+ simply comes with a tested profile in the Filo app, so there is nothing to tune.

What is the biggest thing I can print?

Anything up to 220 × 220 × 250 mm in one piece, about the size of a shoebox standing on its end. Larger models can be split into parts and glued.

How loud is it in an HDB flat?

About 48 dB in quiet mode, similar to a quiet fridge. Most owners print in the living room or a study, and quiet mode is fine to leave running overnight.

What happens if the power trips mid-print?

Filo One saves its position. When power returns, it asks whether to resume, so a long print isn’t lost.

Is it safe to use around kids?

Yes, with an adult nearby. The nozzle reaches 300°C and the bed 100°C, so keep hands away while it prints. The Family Kit includes a printable safety checklist for children.

What maintenance does it need?

Wipe the build plate with isopropyl alcohol every few prints and keep filament dry. A spare 0.4 mm nozzle and tool kit are in the box for the occasional clog.

What if something breaks?

Every Filo One has a 12-month warranty. Message our Shopee store and we’ll arrange a repair or replacement in Singapore, not overseas.
