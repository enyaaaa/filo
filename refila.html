Four steps

How Refila works

Get a bag

Free when you join Filo Club, and included with every printer bundle.

Fill it

PLA scraps and empty spools. A full bag holds about 1 kg.

Post it back

Every bag comes with a prepaid label. No postage to pay.

Earn

Refila weighs your bag and your points land within 7 days.

What goes in the bag

Scraps in. Points out.

1 full bag of PLA scraps + 2 Filo spools ≈ 90 points, about $2.25 back. 200 points = a $5 Shopee voucher.

Accepted

  • PLA & PLA+ scraps 50 pts / kg Failed prints, support pieces and the leftover bits you peel off, any brand.
  • Empty Filo spools 20 pts each Cleaned and refilled, so they’re reused, not recycled.
  • Other plastic spools 10 pts each Sorted for plastic recycling. Cardboard spools go in your blue bin.

Not yet accepted

  • Other plastics (PETG, ABS, TPU, nylon) Not yet. We’ll add these once the PLA stream is running smoothly.
  • Resin prints Resin can’t be remelted. Follow your resin’s disposal guide.
  • Painted or glued prints Paint and glue contaminate the plastic. Remove magnets and inserts too.

Where it goes

No vague green promises.

Recycling claims are easy to make and hard to check. Here’s exactly what Refila does with your returns.

  • Spools: cleaned by Refila and refilled by Filo Print, so they’re reused rather than recycled.
  • PLA scraps: sorted by colour, shredded and melted by Refila into new filament.
  • Reporting: every quarter, Filo Print and Refila publish how many kilograms were collected and where the material went.

Refila questions

Before you send a bag.

What counts as a scrap?

Failed prints, support pieces and the leftover bits you peel off, made from PLA or PLA+ of any brand. Leave out painted or glued prints, resin and other plastics.

Do I need a Filo printer to use Refila?

No. Refila takes PLA scraps and plastic spools from any printer. You just need a free Filo Club account so we can credit your points.

How do I get my Filo Points?

Refila weighs and sorts your bag when it arrives, and we credit your points within 7 days. 200 points can be swapped for a $5 Shopee voucher on the Rewards page.

What happens to my scraps?

Filo spools are cleaned and refilled. PLA scraps are sorted by colour, shredded and melted into new filament. We publish the totals every quarter.
