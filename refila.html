Filo Print × Refila · recycling partnership

Bag it. Return it. Get rewarded.

Filo Print has teamed up with Refila, a recycling partner that collects what your hobby leaves behind: empty spools, supports and failed prints. You earn Filo Points for every return, and we tell you exactly what happens next.

How Refila works

Get a bag

Refila Bags come free with the Starter and Maker Kits, or you can request one through our sign-up form.

Fill it

Collect PLA scraps and empty spools. A full bag holds around 1 kg of scraps.

Send it

Drop it at a partner collection point or post it back. Maker Kits include a prepaid label.

Earn

Refila weighs and sorts your bag, and we credit Filo Points to your account within 7 days.

What goes in the bag?

PLA & PLA+ scraps

Supports, brims, rafts, purge lines and failed prints, from any brand.

Empty Filo spools

Refila cleans and inspects them, then we refill them, so they're reused, not recycled.

Other brands' plastic spools

Accepted and sorted for plastic recycling. No points for cardboard spools; recycle those in your blue bin.

PETG, ABS, TPU, nylon

These need different processing. Refila will add them once the PLA stream is running smoothly.

Resin prints

Resin can't be remelted. Please follow your resin's disposal instructions.

Painted or glued prints

Paint and glue contaminate the material. Remove metal inserts, magnets and stickers too.

Where it goes

No vague green promises.

Recycling claims are easy to make and hard to check. Here’s exactly what Refila does with your returns.

  • Spools: cleaned by Refila and refilled by Filo Print, so they’re reused rather than recycled.
  • PLA scraps: sorted by colour, shredded and re-extruded by Refila into recycled filament.
  • Reporting: every quarter, Filo Print and Refila publish how many kilograms were collected and where the material went.
