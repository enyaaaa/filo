How a drawer of failed prints became Filo.

Anyone who owns a 3D printer knows the drawer: half-finished prints, tangled supports and empty spools that are “too useful to throw away”. We started Filo Print to take that drawer back, with better filament and a way to send the scraps back.

Then we noticed something. Most people asking us about filament didn’t own a printer yet, and the ones who did often stopped using it after a few weeks. They didn’t need more plastic. They needed a printer that just works and a reason to keep printing.

So now we sell the whole thing: three self-levelling printers, the filament to feed them, and a new tested project every week. And when a print fails, or a printer retires, Refila and trade-in bring it back instead of leaving it in a drawer.
