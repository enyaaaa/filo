Our story

It started with a drawer full of failed prints.

Anyone who owns a 3D printer knows the drawer: half-finished prints, tangled supports and empty spools that are “too useful to throw away”. We started Filo Print to take that drawer back.

Then we noticed something. Most people asking us about filament didn’t own a printer yet. So now we sell the whole thing: Filo One, the filament to feed it, and a new reason to print every week.

Who’s behind Filo

We print everything before we sell it.

Filo is run by a small team of makers. Every model, print time and cost you see on this site comes from our own test prints on Filo One, not a manufacturer’s spec sheet.
