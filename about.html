Our story

It started with a drawer full of failed prints.

Anyone who owns a 3D printer knows the drawer: half-finished prints, tangled supports and empty spools that are “too useful to throw away”. We built Filo to give that drawer somewhere to go, and to make the printing that fills it a lot more fun.

Our mission

Make 3D printing approachable

Choosing filament shouldn't take a forum thread. We explain things in plain English and lead with what you can make.

Our promise

Consistent, honest product

One well-tested PLA+, published settings and real tolerances. If a roll lets you down, message us and we'll make it right.

Our commitment

Close the loop, transparently

We take back what we can, say clearly what we can't yet, and report our numbers every quarter.

Why we sell through Shopee

You already have an account, saved addresses, ShopeePay and a pile of vouchers. Building our own checkout wouldn't make your life easier. So we built this site for what Shopee listings can't do:

  • Help you choose with the Filament Finder and compatibility guide
  • Give you tested projects to print the day your order arrives
  • Run Refila take-back and rewards, paid out as Shopee voucher codes
