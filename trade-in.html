How trade-in works

What we accept

Every printer gets a second life.

Working printers go to school makerspaces. Broken ones are stripped for motors, rails and belts, and the plastic goes to Refila.

  • Any FDM printer Any brand or age, working or not, as long as the frame is there.
  • Not resin printers Leftover resin needs special handling, so we can’t collect them yet.
  • Your scraps too Add a full Refila Bag to the box and earn Filo Points for it.
Before you trade in

Trade-in questions, answered.

Can I trade in a printer that isn’t a Filo?

Yes. We accept any FDM printer from any brand. Most trade-ins are older printers from other brands.

Does my old printer need to work?

No. Broken or incomplete printers get half the credit, because they can only be stripped for parts.

Can I use the credit on a bundle?

Yes. The voucher works on any Filo printer or bundle in our Shopee store. It’s one trade-in per new printer.

Do I need to pack the old printer?

Any sturdy box works. Remove the filament and the build plate if it comes off, and our courier takes care of the rest.

Is trade-in available outside Singapore?

Not yet. Collection is only available at Singapore addresses.
