1. What personal data we collect

When you join Filo Club or contact us, we collect:

  • Identity and contact data: your name and email address.
  • Delivery data: your postal code, used to post your Refila Bag.
  • Optional preference data: your printer brand, interests, Refill Plan choice and any message you send us.
  • Club activity: returns received, Filo Points balance, challenge stamps and photos you submit, vouchers issued and any Shopee order numbers you choose to register.

We do not collect NRIC numbers, payment card details or passwords for Shopee. All payments happen on Shopee.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and manage your Filo Club account and Filo Points.
  • To send your starter pack, welcome voucher and Refila Bag.
  • To process returned materials, award challenge stamps and issue reward voucher codes.
  • To email your Refill Plan vouchers, only if you chose a Refill Plan.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you have separately opted in.

3. Consent and withdrawal

We collect, use and disclose your personal data only with your consent, given through the checkbox on our sign-up form, and only for the purposes we told you about. Consent to marketing is separate and never pre-ticked.

You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing dpo@filo.sg. We will act on your request within 10 working days. If you withdraw consent for account purposes, we may no longer be able to run your Filo Club account or issue rewards.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Our form-processing and email service providers, so we can receive your sign-up and send emails.
  • Our courier or postal partner, to deliver your Refila Bag. They receive only your name and address details.

If a service provider stores data outside Singapore, we require it to protect your data to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

5. How we protect and retain it

Our forms are submitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Only authorised team members can access member data, and only for the purposes above. We keep your personal data while your Filo Club account is active, and delete or anonymise it within 12 months after you close your account or it has been inactive for 24 months, unless we're legally required to keep it longer.

6. Access and correction

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, how it has been used or disclosed in the past year, and ask us to correct any errors. Email dpo@filo.sg and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Marketing messages

We only send marketing emails if you tick the optional marketing box. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We do not send marketing SMS or calls to Singapore telephone numbers.

8. Cookies and Shopee

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Links to Shopee include campaign tags (UTM parameters) that tell us which page a visit came from. These tags contain no personal data. Once you're on Shopee, Shopee's own privacy policy applies.

9. Contact our Data Protection Officer

Data Protection Officer, Filo
Email: dpo@filo.sg

If you're not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) of Singapore.
